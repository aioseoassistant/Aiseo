# Home Calendar User Guide

This guide is for end users who will be using the home calendar display. No technical knowledge required!

## First Time Setup

### Step 1: Power On

1. Plug in the power adapter
2. The screen will light up (may take 30-60 seconds)
3. You'll see a loading screen, then the setup wizard

### Step 2: Connect to WiFi

1. **WiFi Setup Screen** will appear automatically
2. You'll see a list of available WiFi networks
3. **Tap your network name** from the list
4. **Enter your WiFi password**
5. Tap **Connect**
6. Wait 5-10 seconds for connection
7. Screen will show "Connected!" and move to next step

**Troubleshooting**:
- **Network not showing?** Tap "Refresh Networks"
- **Wrong password?** Tap "Back" and try again
- **Still not working?** Check your WiFi router is working

### Step 3: Add Your Calendars

After WiFi is connected, you'll see the **Calendar Setup** screen.

#### Option A: Google Calendar

1. Tap **"Add Google Calendar"**
2. You'll see a **QR code** and a **6-digit code**
3. On your phone or computer:
   - Open the URL shown (or scan QR code)
   - You'll be taken to Google login
   - Sign in to your Google account
   - Grant calendar access permission
   - Enter the 6-digit code if prompted
4. Back on the calendar display:
   - Wait 5-10 seconds
   - You'll see "Calendar added successfully!"
   - Your calendar name will appear in the list

#### Option B: Apple iCloud Calendar

1. Tap **"Add iCloud Calendar"**
2. On your phone/computer, get your calendar URL:
   - **iPhone/iPad**:
     - Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → iCloud.com
     - Open Safari → icloud.com
     - Open Calendar app
     - Click gear icon → Settings
     - Click on your calendar name
     - Check "Public Calendar"
     - Copy the URL
   - **Mac**:
     - Open Calendar app
     - Right-click your calendar → Share Calendar
     - Make public and copy URL
3. Back on calendar display:
   - Paste or type the URL
   - Tap **"Add Calendar"**

#### Option C: Other CalDAV Calendars

1. Tap **"Add CalDAV Calendar"**
2. Enter:
   - **Server URL**: Your CalDAV server address
   - **Username**: Your username
   - **Password**: Your password
3. Tap **"Add Calendar"**

#### Adding Multiple Calendars

You can add calendars from multiple people in your household:
1. Repeat the steps above for each person
2. Each calendar will be color-coded
3. All events will appear together on the display

### Step 4: Finish Setup

1. After adding all calendars, tap **"Finish Setup"**
2. Calendar will load and display all your events
3. Setup is complete!

│                                      │

**Color Coding**:
- Each person's calendar has a different color
- Events show the event name and time
- All-day events appear at the top

### Calendar Updates

The calendar automatically refreshes every **5 minutes** to show new events.

**Manual Refresh**:
- Tap anywhere on the screen
- Swipe down to refresh immediately

### Navigation

**Touch Controls** (if you have a touchscreen):
- **Tap** to wake from sleep
- **Swipe left**: Next week
- **Swipe right**: Previous week
- **Swipe down**: Refresh now
- **Pinch**: (Future feature for month view)

**Automatic**:
- Returns to current week after 60 seconds of inactivity

1. **Tap and hold** for 3 seconds

### Changing Calendar Colors

1. **Tap and hold** for 3 seconds
2. Select **"Manage Calendars"**

## Troubleshooting

### Calendar Not Showing Events

**Check**:
1. Is WiFi connected? (Look for WiFi icon in corner)
2. Are calendars added? (Settings → Manage Calendars)
3. Tap screen to refresh manually
4. Restart: Hold power button (if accessible) or unplug and replug

### Events Are Old/Outdated

1. **Swipe down** to force refresh
2. Check WiFi connection
3. Check that your source calendar (Google, iCloud) has the latest events

### WiFi Disconnected

1. Tap and hold → Settings → WiFi
2. Tap **"Reconnect"**
3. If that fails, tap **"Change Network"** and reconnect

### Screen Is Blank

1. **Tap** the screen (might be in sleep mode)
2. Check power cable is connected
3. Unplug and replug power
4. Wait 60 seconds for reboot

### Touch Not Working

1. Use a **soft cloth** to clean screen
2. Try tapping with finger tip (not nail)
3. Restart device by unplugging power
4. If still not working, contact support

### Events Missing

**Check**:
1. Calendar source (Google, iCloud) - are events there?
2. Date range settings (Settings → Show Events From)
3. Event filters (Settings → Filters)
4. Calendar is not hidden (Settings → Manage Calendars)

### Colors Are Wrong

1. Tap and hold → Settings → Manage Calendars
2. Check each calendar's color
3. Change colors as needed
4. Tap **"Save"**

### Display Is Too Bright/Dim

1. Tap and hold → Settings → Display
2. Adjust **Brightness** slider
3. Tap **"Save"**

## Maintenance

### Regular Maintenance

**Weekly**:
- Clean screen with soft, dry microfiber cloth
- Check calendar is showing current events

**Monthly**:
- Restart device (unplug and replug)
- Check for software updates (automatic in background)

### Cleaning

**Screen**:
- Use microfiber cloth (like glasses cloth)
- For stubborn marks: slightly dampen cloth with water
- **NEVER** use cleaning chemicals or alcohol
- **NEVER** spray liquid directly on screen

**Frame/Housing**:
- Wipe with dry cloth
- Dust air vents (if any)

### Power Management

**During the Day**:
- Leave powered on
- Calendar will auto-sleep screen after 30 minutes

**When Away** (vacation, etc.):
- Unplug from power
- Replug when back - calendar will auto-start

**Power Outages**:
- Calendar will automatically restart when power returns
- May take 60-90 seconds to boot
- Will reconnect to WiFi automatically

## Privacy & Security

### What Data Is Stored

**On the device**:
- WiFi password (encrypted)
- Calendar URLs/tokens
- Your preferences and settings

**NOT stored**:
- Full calendar event contents (only cached temporarily)
- Personal information beyond calendars

### Data Sync

- Calendar only **reads** your calendars
- It **cannot** create, edit, or delete events
- It only sees calendars you explicitly add

### Revoking Access

**Google Calendar**:
1. Go to myaccount.google.com
2. Security → Third-party access
3. Find "Home Calendar Display"
4. Click **Remove access**

**iCloud**:
1. Go to calendar settings
2. Unshare or make calendar private again

## FAQ

**Q: Can I add my work calendar?**
A: Yes! Follow the same steps to add Google, iCloud, or CalDAV calendars.

**Q: Will other people see my private events?**
A: Only calendars you explicitly add will be shown. Don't add calendars with private events if you don't want others to see them.

**Q: Can I hide certain events?**
A: Yes, use filters in Settings. Or mark events as "Private" in your source calendar.

**Q: How many calendars can I add?**
A: Up to 10 calendars. More than that may slow down the display.

**Q: Does it work without internet?**
A: It will show the last synced events, but won't update until WiFi reconnects.

**Q: Can I use it in landscape or portrait?**
A: Yes! The installer can configure orientation. Default is landscape.

**Q: What happens if WiFi password changes?**
A: Calendar will show WiFi setup screen. Reconnect with new password.

**Q: Can I customize colors and fonts?**
A: Yes, in Settings → Display → Customize.

**Q: Does it support multiple languages?**
A: Currently English only. Future updates may add more languages.

**Q: How do I reset to factory settings?**
A: Tap and hold → Settings → Advanced → **Reset to Defaults**. This will erase all calendar connections and settings.
